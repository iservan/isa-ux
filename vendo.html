Vendo · Fraud management

Making regulatory limits visible before they become penalties.

Vendo is a corporate payment processor that helps online merchants, including high-risk ones, get paid securely in a heavily regulated space. Its platform combines reliable payment infrastructure with AI-enabled tools for pricing, fraud prevention, and risk monitoring, giving clients clear data they can act on.

01 · The problem

In regulated financial products, businesses shouldn’t find out they’ve exceeded a regulatory limit after the fact. We built a risk-monitoring experience that turns complex card-network rules into something merchants can read at a glance, investigate by source, and act on early. The goal was a solution users could trust with high-stakes data, and was easy to track.

02 · The key insight

They needed to see their distance from the regulatory limit, not just a number.

The job was not to report chargebacks. It was to show how much room was left, and where the chargebacks were coming from.

03 · Design

The design solution: a reporting suite that helps users to move from risk to revenue

We built risk monitoring report tools, to help the clients see how close they were to exceeding the regulatory limit while there was still time to act. So the screen was built around one question: How much room do I have left?

That question shaped everything: a gauge that shows the distance to the regulatory limit, filters that trace where a problem starts, and a trend that shows which way things are heading.

A gauge, not just a percentage

The gauge turns a rule into something you can read at a glance. The users see where they sit relative to the regulatory limits and how much room they have left.

The filters

The filters (card brand, device, site, traffic source, location) let users find where the problem is coming from, not just that there is one.

The trend

A table that shows whether things are improving or slipping, showing monthly variations.

The Risk report was a part of a larger suite of reports that shared the same design system and layout, but with different questions and data.

05 · Outcome

Regulated limits became something people could see coming.

Clients could see where they stood against the Visa and Mastercard limits, trace a problem back to its source with the filters, and act while there was still time. The same patterns (the gauge, the filters, the trend) gave every risk metric a shared language, so the data stayed clear and trustworthy.
