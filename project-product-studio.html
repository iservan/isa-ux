Product Studio

AI Product Management Tool

  • The Problem

Project managers waste a lot of time writing specs by hand and guessing what to build next. The specs lack real context from the codebase, stakeholders and customer calls. Engineers get confused, and costly rework shows up too late.

  • The Solution

We built and launched Product Studio, an intelligence layer that helps teams predict what to build next. It is an AI conversational workflow that turns a rough idea into a build-ready spec, grounded in the actual codebase, customer feedback, and delivery metrics. Teams plan 4x faster and spend 75% less on AI tokens.

MY ROLE

I led the UX research, conceptualization UX/UI design of Product Studio, collaborating with product, enegenieer and marketing stakeholders. My main focus was figuring out how to create conversational experiences that feel trustworthy and simple to use.

  • The process

  • UX & UI design

I worked closely with project managers through user interviews to understand their needs and turn ideas into practical product flows. I built and maintained a unified Design System in Figma and Vue 3, connecting design tokens, reusable components, and production implementation.

We looked at how product managers write specs today. Then we designed an AI flow that feels fast, clear, and trustworthy. Every screen had to answer one question: can I trust this enough to act on it?

  • Ideation

User personas

We mapped three user personas, each interacting with the tool in a different way. While they all used the same AI workflow, each had different needs. We then compared these assumptions with actual usage data from our analytics tools to see if the behavior matched what we had learned through research.

It has made it easier to evolve our design system by testing existing components and identifying where they may need to be adapted or extended, while making engineering handoffs much more precise.
