Market Intelligence Platform Launch

  • The Problem

Real estate professionals lack accessible, real-time market intelligence to make confident investment and development decisions. Complex data fragmentation and multiple tool switching create friction in the decision-making workflow, limiting insights accessibility across teams.

  • The Outcome

We launched REalyse Pulse, a unified market intelligence platform designed from the ground up through collaborative research and user-centered design. By synthesizing fragmented data into actionable insights and enabling multi-role workflows, we achieved rapid user adoption (73%) and sustained engagement. The successful 0→1 launch validated our market thesis and established Pulse as the foundation for REalyse's platform expansion, enabling product growth.

  • Research & Discovery

I conducted comprehensive market research and user interviews to validate Pulse's market opportunity and understand the fragmented workflows of real estate professionals. These insights shaped the platform's core value proposition and confirmed the need for unified market intelligence.

Research & Insights

Working with the go-to-market and product teams, I conducted in-depth interviews with 18 potential users including real estate developers, institutional lenders, and investment consultants. These sessions uncovered key workflow friction points and data gaps that shaped Pulse’s roadmap.

I also performed competitive analysis benchmarking 5 real estate platforms, market intelligence platforms, identifying gaps in usability, data integration, and multi-role workflows. Combined with user feedback, this validated our differentiation and confirmed a clear market opportunity.

  • Key findings
Finding 1
Real-time market alerts and trend detection significantly improve the speed and confidence of investment decisions
Finding 2
Users value reliable, transparent data more than having many features, and need clear signals they can trust the data
Finding 3
Multi-role teams (analysts, managers, executives) need customized views of data that match their decision-making needs, not one-size-fits-all dashboards
Main takeaway - Disconnected data slows decisions.
Real estate professionals were spending 8+ hours weekly consolidating market data from across multiple tools or sources. The cognitive load of managing multiple platforms created decision delays and missed opportunities. Users consistently requested a unified platform where all critical market intelligence was accessible in minutes.

  • Strategy & Personas

Based on research findings, I defined the platform's strategic pillars and created four distinct user personas representing different roles and needs within real estate organizations. These personas guided all subsequent design decisions and helped the team maintain focus on user value.

Platform Strategic Pillars

Using insights from user research and competitive analysis, I worked with the product team to define three core strategic pillars that would differentiate Pulse in a crowded market:

1. Unified Intelligence: Consolidate fragmented market data into a single source of truth. 2. Role-Based Experiences: Tailor information architecture and visualizations to each user's decision-making needs. 3. Data Transparency: Build trust through clear data provenance, methodology, and confidence indicators.

User personas

I created four distinct user personas representing different roles who would interact with Pulse daily. These personas helped the team empathize with users and aligned design decisions to user motivations, goals, and pain points.

  • Market Analyst
  • Research and analyst roles who prepare market reports and competitive intelligence for decision makers
  • Goal Synthesize complex market data into clear insights and reports
  • Needs Advanced filtering, export capabilities, and pre-built report templates
  • Value 60% faster report generation through unified data and templating
  • Portfolio Manager
  • Decision makers who evaluate investment opportunities and track portfolio performance against market trends
  • Goal Make confident investment decisions based on current market conditions
  • Needs Real-time alerts, trend detection, and customizable dashboards
  • Value Actionable market alerts that surface opportunities minutes faster than competitors
  • Executive Stakeholder
  • C-level and senior leaders responsible for strategic direction and market opportunity assessment
  • Goal Understand macro market trends and portfolio health at a glance
  • Needs Executive dashboards with key metrics, KPI tracking, and strategic insights
  • Value 2-minute market briefing replacing hours of data consolidation

  • Prototyping & Validation

Low-Fidelity Wireframes & User Testing

I partnered closely with engineering and data science teams to rapidly prototype core user flows. Using low-fidelity wireframes, we validated information architecture decisions and core interactions with 6 test users before committing to high-fidelity design. This approach accelerated decision-making and reduced rework downstream in the development cycle.

Key validation sessions tested: (1) Information hierarchy and dashboard layouts for different roles, (2) Mental models for data filtering and exploration, (3) Alert notification patterns and frequency preferences, (4) Compare and benchmark workflows.

Key Insight from Testing

Users, when assessing property value, rely heavily on comparable home prices, matching size, condition, and features in the same area. Identifying Comparables proved to be the most critical step in generating a REalyse valuation report. It needed to be visual, and users wanted to verify the characteristics of each property.

  • Product Design & Scaling

High-Fidelity Design & Design System

Real-time market alerts and trend detection

Built on insights from user testing, I designed dashboard experience for portfolio managers see investment-focused metrics and alerts, analysts see data export and filtering tools, high-level KPIs and portfolio health summaries—all from the same underlying data.

Finding 1 in Action

Real-time market alerts and trend detection significantly improve the speed and confidence of investment decisions

Unified Data Intelligence

I designed the data exploration experience to surface market insights intuitively. Advanced filtering, comparative analysis, and trend visualization tools enable users to discover patterns without context switching between platforms. Transparent data provenance indicators build confidence in recommendations.

Findings 1 & 3 in Action

Unified data access eliminates tool switching, while data transparency builds decision confidence (Finding 2). Users spend 60% less time data consolidation compared to previous workflows.

Market Alerts & Real-Time Intelligence

Building on Finding 3, I designed an intelligent alert system that surfaces market opportunities and anomalies in real-time. Users customize alert thresholds and receive notifications when market conditions match their investment criteria, enabling faster decision-making.

Finding 3 in Action

Real-time alerts surface opportunities before competitors, giving users measurable competitive advantage. Early adopters reported capturing 3x more investment opportunities in the first month post-launch.

Design System & Team Scaling

To support rapid iteration post-launch, I built a comprehensive design system documenting component patterns, interaction principles, and data visualization standards. This enabled the design team to scale from 1 designer to 3 as the company grew, while maintaining visual and interaction consistency.
